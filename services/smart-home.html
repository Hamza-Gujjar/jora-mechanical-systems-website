Smart Thermostats & Home Comfort Controls

Smarter controls can make your heating and cooling more comfortable and efficient. Jora Mechanical Systems installs and configures smart thermostats and zoning for compatible systems.

What we offer

  • Smart and programmable thermostat installation
  • Compatibility check with your equipment
  • Zoning for multi-level homes
  • Remote control by phone
  • Setup walk-through

Smart thermostats

A smart thermostat learns your schedule, lets you adjust from your phone and can reduce wasted heating and cooling. Not every system works with every thermostat, so we check wiring and compatibility first.

Zoning

Homes with hot upstairs rooms or cold basements often benefit from zoning, which uses dampers and multiple thermostats to send comfort where it is needed.

Setup and support

We install, configure and show you how to use it, so your system is set up properly from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Will a smart thermostat save me money?

Savings depend on your habits, home and equipment. Setting back temperatures when you are away or asleep is where most savings come from.

Can you install a thermostat I already bought?

Usually yes, after checking it is compatible with your system.
