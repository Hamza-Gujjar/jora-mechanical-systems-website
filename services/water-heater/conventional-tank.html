Conventional Water Heater Installation

Conventional tank water heaters are affordable, dependable and simple to maintain. Jora Mechanical Systems replaces failed tanks quickly and cleanly.

What we offer

  • Gas and electric tank models
  • Common tank sizes for most households
  • Fast replacement
  • Old tank removal
  • Code-compliant installation

Choosing a size

Household size and usage determine the right tank. Too small runs out of hot water; too large wastes energy.

Replacement process

We shut off and drain the old unit, set the new tank, make water, gas or electrical connections, check venting and test.

Maintenance

Flushing and checking the anode rod can extend the tank's life.

Frequently asked questions

How long does replacement take?

Often a few hours for a standard swap.

Should I repair or replace a leaking tank?

Leaks from the tank itself cannot be repaired. Replacement is the safe solution.
